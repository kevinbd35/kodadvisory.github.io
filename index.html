Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of businesses does KOD Advisory work with? +

KOD Advisory works with middle-market businesses across Insurance, Energy, Real Estate Development, Technology, Distribution, Manufacturing, and Alcoholic Beverage industries. We specialize in companies navigating change — whether through M&A transactions, turnarounds, capital raises, or performance improvement initiatives.

What is turnaround management consulting? +

Turnaround management involves analyzing a distressed or underperforming business to identify root causes, restructure operations, realign costs, and restore profitability. KOD Advisory provides turnaround advisory and implementation services including interim executive leadership, cash flow stabilization, and operational restructuring.

When does a business need an interim CFO? +

A business typically needs an interim CFO during a transaction (M&A, capital raise), a turnaround, rapid growth, or when transitioning between permanent CFOs. KOD Advisory provides part-time and interim CFO, CEO, and COO services to businesses that need institutional-grade financial leadership without a full-time executive cost.

What does buy-side M&A advisory involve? +

Buy-side M&A advisory involves helping an acquirer identify, evaluate, and execute an acquisition. KOD Advisory provides M&A strategy, due diligence, value creation planning, post-acquisition integration, and tax strategy for transaction structuring — representing the buyer's interests throughout the process.

What does sell-side M&A advisory involve? +

Sell-side advisory prepares a business owner to maximize value in a sale. KOD Advisory assists with improving financial information accuracy, building the data room, augmenting management resources, and structuring the transaction for optimal tax efficiency.

How does KOD Advisory help with capital structuring? +

KOD Advisory helps businesses determine and employ the most effective capital balance for their situation. Through global networks, we provide consulting on sourcing capital for transactions, equity and debt placements, positioning clients to access the right capital at the right terms.

Is KOD Advisory a CPA firm? +

KOD Advisory LLC is led by Kevin B. Dunham, a multi-state licensed CPA (FL, NY & TX) and former partner at top CPA firms. The firm provides business advisory, transaction advisory, and tax structuring consulting. For attest and audit services, clients work with licensed CPA firms in coordination with KOD Advisory.
